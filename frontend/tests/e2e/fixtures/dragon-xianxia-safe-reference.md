# Safe Reference Fixture For Three-Chapter Demo

Mist gathered below the ferry steps while the mountain bell sounded once and then refused to fade.

The apprentice hid the cracked jade token in his sleeve, counting breaths until the river answered.

A lantern drifted upstream against the current, carrying no flame but casting shadows on every face.

When the gate examiner smiled, the crowd understood that the easy path had already been withdrawn.

The weakest initiate stepped forward first, and the furnace fire bent away as if embarrassed by its own heat.

No elder explained the rule; the courtyard learned it from the silence after the copper gong.

The oath stone stayed cold until someone chose duty over speed, then rang beneath the moon.

At dawn, a red thread appeared on the token, pointing beyond the sect toward a rival valley.
